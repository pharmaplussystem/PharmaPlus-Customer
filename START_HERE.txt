Includes login/create account, working cart, left-side customer features, live order statuses, full order details, rejection reasons, retail/wholesale pricing, optional prescription upload, pickup/home delivery, Google Maps live location, and payment-before-send workflow.

Payment note: the portal collects the customer's payment method and transaction message/reference; it does not independently verify MTN/Airtel/bank/Visa payments. The pharmacy must verify payment before fulfilling the order.
